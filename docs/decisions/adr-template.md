## Context

Describes the circumstances, constraints, and requirements around this decision. This can be anything that goes into the decision making process, including architectural, technological, product, business, design, and project-specific factors.

## Decision

Describes what we will do as a response to the situation described by the context. This is written in active voice: "We will..."

## Consequences

Describes what changes - good, bad, and neutral - happen as a result of implementing this decision.

## Alternatives Considered

Describes alternative approaches that we rejected, with brief descriptions of why.

## Other Advice

Describes other advice we received that didn't make it into the "Decision" or "Alternatives Considered". This may take the form of links out to Slack, PRs, etc. as convenient, so long as the source is permanent. If all the advice is accounted for above, this can be omitted.

## Resources

_Optional_. Additional documents and materials that would be helpful to the reader.

## Revision History

- YYYY-MM-DD: This describes changes to the document and the date they were made. It's a list of revisions with dates formatted as YYYY-MM-DD. Make an entry here with each new PR that makes changes to the decision.
